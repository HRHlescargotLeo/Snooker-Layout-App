Dimensions used
  • Playing area: 3,569 × 1,778 mm (11 ft 8½ in × 5 ft 10 in)
  • Balls: 52.5 mm diameter
  • Baulk line: 737 mm from the bottom cushion face
  • D: semicircle of 292 mm radius on the baulk line
  • Brown, yellow, green: centre and ends of the D; yellow on the right from baulk
  • Blue: centre spot. Pink: midway between blue and top cushion
  • Black: 324 mm from the top cushion face
  • Reds: apex as close behind the pink as possible without touching
  • Corner pockets: 89 mm at the fall, jaws cut along the pocket diagonal
  • Middle pockets: 108 mm at the fall, rounded jaws flaring to the nose
  • Cushion rubber: 50 mm from nose to rail
  • Playing area: 1,829 × 914 mm (6 ft × 3 ft, the 7 ft league table)
  • Object balls: 50.8 mm (2 in). Cue ball: 47.6 mm (1⅞ in)
  • Baulk line: one fifth of the length, 366 mm from the bottom cushion
  • Black spot: where the diagonals from the corner to the middle pockets cross, 1,372 mm from the bottom cushion
  • Rack: black on the black spot, in the middle of the third row
  • Cue ball: starts in baulk, played from anywhere behind the baulk line
  • Pockets: 81 mm between the jaws (1.6 × ball diameter), rounded like snooker pockets
  • Cushion rubber: 40 mm from nose to rail

Keyboard: with a ball selected, arrow keys nudge it 1 mm (Shift for 10 mm), Delete takes it off the table, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Set up from photos

  • Take one to three photos with all four corner pockets in the frame.
  • Best results: one photo from each end of the table, with the phone held up high.
  • A third photo from the side helps when balls are bunched together.

Drag each marker to the corner of the cloth, where the two cushions meet inside the pocket. If the ends are the wrong way round, use Turn.
